4-Stage Quality Assurance

Every single piece is inspected before it reaches your retail shelf.

1. Fabric Audit

Checking GSM, shrinkage, and color fastness before cutting.

2. Inline Inspection

Monitoring stitch density (SPI) and pattern matching during assembly.

3. End-of-Line Check

Checking for loose threads, button security, and measurement specs.

4. Packaging Audit

Ensuring correct tagging, folding, and master carton sealing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Our standard MOQ is 50 pieces per style/color. This includes a complete size set ratio (usually M, L, XL, XXL).

Do you provide custom brand labels?

Yes! For orders above 200 pieces, we can manufacture shirts with your custom woven tags, wash care labels, and branded packaging at a nominal extra cost.

How long does shipping take?

In-stock catalog items are dispatched within 48 hours. Custom manufacturing orders take 14-21 days depending on fabric availability and order size. We ship Pan-India via reliable transport partners.
